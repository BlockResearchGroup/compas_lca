"Helpful cues & typical ready-mixed concrete properties (examples, not a mandatory list):\n"
"- Classes & performance:\n"
"  • strength_class (string/enum; e.g., C25/30, C30/37)\n"
"  • consistency_class (enum; slump S1–S5, flow F1–F6, or V classes)\n"
"  • exposure_classes (enum[multi] or string; e.g., XC, XD, XS, XF, XA, XM lists)\n"
"  • chloride_class (string; e.g., Cl 0.1)\n"
"- Mix design / constituents:\n"
"  • cement_type (enum; CEM I/II/III/IV/V with subtypes, or ASTM types)\n"
"  • binder_content_kg_m3 (float, kg/m³)\n"
"  • scm_type (enum; e.g., ggbs, fly_ash, silica_fume, limestone)\n"
"  • scm_fraction_pct (float, % of binder)\n"
"  • water_cement_ratio (float, unitless) or water_binder_ratio (float)\n"
"  • admixtures (enum; superplasticizer, retarder, accelerator, air_entrainer, wra; optionally boolean flags)\n"
"  • air_content_pct (float, %)\n"
"- Aggregates & density:\n"
"  • max_aggregate_size_mm (integer, mm)\n"
"  • aggregate_type (enum; natural, recycled, lightweight, heavyweight)\n"
"  • recycled_aggregate_pct (float, %)\n"
"  • density_class (enum; lightweight, normal_weight, heavyweight)\n"
"  • fresh_density_kg_m3 (float, kg/m³) and/or hardened_density_kg_m3 (float, kg/m³)\n"
