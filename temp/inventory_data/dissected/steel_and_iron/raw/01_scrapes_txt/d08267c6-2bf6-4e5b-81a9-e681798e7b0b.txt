| Technical purpose of product or process | The TRM pile system is used as a full displacement pile for the foundation of buildings, for the foundation of engineering structures (such as bridges, wind power and photovoltaic plants, noise barriers, pipelines, masts) and for the construction of slope and excavation protection. The TRM pile, which is driven into the ground, effectively transfers the forces from the structure into the ground. The TRM pile can also be used with fill concrete with or without grouting of the shell. The present EPD deals with the driving of the pile tubes into the ground without consideration of concrete filling or jacket grouting. Translated with www.DeepL.com/Translator (free version) |

| Technology description including background system | TRM pile systems are ductile centrifugally cast pile tubes (ductile cast iron) with an insertion end and a socket that can be connected together to form piles of any length. The socket and the insertion end allow the pile elements to be connected securely and easily to form a ductile pile. When the pile tubes are driven into the ground, they form a rigid connection to absorb centric forces and bending moments. The ductile driven pile of the TRM pile system is usually composed of one or more pile tubes (depending on the required length), a pile shoe (ungrouted/grouted) and a self-centring pile head plate. This EPD deals only with the pile tubes themselves without considering pile shoe types, pile head plates, concrete fills and jacket grouting. In this environmental product declaration, the electricity generation is calculated according to the amount of electricity supplied by the Tiroler Wasserkraft AG (TIWAG) to the Tiroler Rohre GmbH. |
