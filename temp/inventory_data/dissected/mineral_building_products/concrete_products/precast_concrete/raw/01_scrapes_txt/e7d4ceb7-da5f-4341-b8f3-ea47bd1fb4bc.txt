| Technology description including background system | The declared products are two-layer multi-coloured concrete paving stones or slaps 8 cm thick. The mass per unit is 180 kg/m². The concrete is mixed using natural aggregates, cement, water and additives. The surface is not treated. The concrete paving stones pictured here correspond to average multi-coloured concrete paving stones or slaps 8 cm thick. The life-cycle analysis includes the production of the concrete paving stones, including the production of raw materials, energy supply, transportation of raw materials to the plant as well as the actual manufacturing. The transportation to construction sites and the usage phase are not incorporated. The disposal phase includes demolition, transportation to processing, recycling and landfilling. Credits and charges outside the system limits encompass the recycling potential. The impact of waste is included in the module where the waste incurs. The machinery, facilities and infrastructure necessary for manufacturing are disregarded. Transportation of auxiliary materials and packaging is not included because of small amounts and therefore small relevance. Technical Data: Data valid corresponding to declarations of performance according to /EN 1338 Concrete paving blocks/, /EN 1339 Concrete paving flags/. This declaration refers to the manufacturing and end of life of 1 m² concrete paving stones or slaps, multi-coloured with face concrete, average mass per unit 180 kg/m² and 8 cm thickness. |
