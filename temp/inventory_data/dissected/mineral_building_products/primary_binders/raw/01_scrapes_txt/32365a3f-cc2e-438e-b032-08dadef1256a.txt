| General comment on data set | Holcim Duo 3 N-LH ist ein Hochofenzement CEM III/A 32,5 N-LH der gemäß EN 197-1 hergestellt und überwacht wird. Dieser Zement ist ein hydraulisch erhärtendes Bindemittel zur Herstellung von Beton und Mörtel. Er besteht aus einem Gemisch fein aufgemahlener, nichtmetallisch-anorganischer Bestandteile. Nach Zugabe von Wasser zum Zement entsteht eine Suspension (Zementleim), die aufgrund einsetzender Hydratationsreaktion sowohl an der Luft als auch unter Wasser erstarrt und erhärtet sowie dauerhaft fest bleibt. Die Zusammensetzung des Produkts entspricht den Vorgaben der EN 197-1. | |||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||

| Technology description including background system | Holcim Duo 3 N-LH is manufactured at the Schwelgern plant. The Portland cement clinker component is mainly supplied by clinker-producing cement works belonging to Holcim (Deutschland) GmbH. Production of Portland cement clinker Limestone, marl, clay or their natural mixture, are required for the production of Portland cement clinker. These starting materials are mined in quarries, pre-crushed and transported to the nearby cement works. There they are homogenized into an intermediate product, with additional natural or secondary corrective substances being added to fine-tune the chemical composition. In the subsequent heating process, the Portland cement clinker is produced from the intermediate product. The firing process takes place in a rotary kiln, where the material is thermally converted at around 1450 °C and then rapidly cooled. The Portland cement clinker is then transported to the Schwelgern plant. Production of blast furnace slag During the production of pig iron in the blast furnace process, liquid blast furnace slag is produced as a by-product at around 1500 °C. The slag is shock-cooled with water to produce a glass-like granulate with latent hydraulic properties. The blast furnace slag is then stored on heaps. Cement Production To produce the ready-to-sell cement Holcim Duo 3 N-LH, the cement components are finely ground and combined. A sulfate carrier is added to control the setting behavior. The Schwelgern plant is ISO 9001 certified. | |||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||
