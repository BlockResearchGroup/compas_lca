| Technology description including background system | Foreground system The system boundaries comprise the cradle to gate life cycle of vegetation substrate made of (mass based):( 80% old porous brick; 10% pumice; 2% peat; 8% grass/greensheet). The LCI dataset consider the production and assembly of the parts. Transportation processes for the raw materials are accounted for as far as data on average transport distances were available. All relevant energy and energy flow are included in the system. In the disposal phase, a mechanical dismantling (excavator) is assumed in module C1. The transport in module C2 to the treatment plant takes place with a truck (50km). In module C3, the material is processed, any reinforcement is fed to a sink (no loads, no credits). A treatment loss of 25% was assumed. This loss is fed to an inert material landfill within Module C3 (average landfill emissions). In Module D, a credit for the net amount of material (excluding reinforcement and treatment losses) for the substitution of natural gravel as backfill material is given. |
