| Technology description including background system | Screed mortar produced in the factory, which is laid on the construction site directly on the subfloor, with or without bonding, or on an intermediate separating or insulating layer, in order to achieve a specified height, to receive a floor covering or to be used immediately. The differentiation is made according to the main binder: calcium sulphate screed. Technical Data: Compressive strength according to EN 13892-2 ≤ 40 N/mm2 Thermal conductivity according to EN 1745 lambda10,dry,mat / P = 50 % ≥ 0.53 W/(mK) Thermal conductivity according to EN 1745 lambda10,dry,mat / P = 90 % ≥ 0,58 W/(mK) Flexural strength according to EN 13892-2 ≤ 10 N/mm2 Water vapour permeability according to EN 1015-19: 15/35 pH value according to EN 13454-2 ≥ 7 Dry bulk density according to EN 1015-10 ≥ 1500 kg/m³ Adhesion shear strength, water absorption, adhesive tensile strength and sound absorption coefficient are not relevant. Declared Unit: 1 kg screed mortar as mineral mortar, product group calcium sulphate screed with a dry bulk density > 1500 kg/m³. |
