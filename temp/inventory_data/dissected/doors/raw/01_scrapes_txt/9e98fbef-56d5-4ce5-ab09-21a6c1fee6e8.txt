| Technical purpose of product or process | Side door (NT 500) for N 500 up-and-over garage door (see EPD-SGT-0.11.1), steel, single-leaf. Door elements single-leaf, appearance as up-and-over garage doors with steel and aluminium infill. Face width 60 mm, frame made of galvanised square tubing (60 × 60 mm), primer coating in door leaf colour. Door leaf fillings made of steel/aluminium or customer-supplied filling possible. Door filling made of steel. The galvanised frame profiles are primed in traffic white (based on RAL 9016) as standard. Version with high-quality powder coating (high-quality primer) on both sides, inside and outside: traffic white, based on RAL 9016. Colour, preferred colours based on RAL colour chart. Fittings and lock: Side door as standard with handle/handle, PZ version, black plastic, lock without profile cylinder (required profile cylinder length 40.5 / 40.5 mm, for motif 405 plus panel thickness), 3D aluminium hinges. The clear passage width is reduced at opening angles of less than 180°. | ||
